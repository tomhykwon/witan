2026 · Oxford

Strategy Witan at Saïd Business School

Thursday, 25 September 2026 · Oxford

The first meeting under the Witan name, hosted by Oxford’s Saïd Business School. The day opened with a panel on the future of strategy scholarship, ran parallel sessions on theoretical and substantive future agendas, and closed with a debate on whether the field of strategy should exist at all.

Sessions

  • Reimagining Strategy Scholarship for a Changing World — opening panel
  • Future Agendas: Theoretical Perspectives — parallel tracks on Behavioral Strategy & Cognition, Institutional Theory & Categories, Strategy as Practice & Process
  • Future Agendas: Substantive Issues — parallel tracks on Governance, Societal Issues, Tech & AI
  • Debate: “Strategy should no longer exist”

A written recap will appear on the blog in late 2026.
