The 2026 Witan closed with an Oxford Union–style debate on a deliberately uncomfortable motion: “This House believes strategy as a field should no longer exist.” The room voted before, during and after. The motion passed.

The case for abolition

The proposition argued that the field has lost its boundaries. Behavioural strategy, platform strategy, strategic entrepreneurship — adding “strategic” to a topic, they said, has become almost meaningless. The result is a field too broad to be a specialty and too insular to be a discipline: “a confederation of clans.”

They also argued that strategy imposes a tax on knowledge. Scholars who borrow from economics, sociology or psychology must repackage what they find to claim a “unique contribution to strategy” — producing relabelled constructs rather than new knowledge. Add an obsession with causal identification that pulls work towards marginal questions, and a record on practical relevance that has underdelivered, and the answer, they said, is to dissolve strategy back into management.

The case for the field

The opposition replied that fragmentation is neither new nor unique. Medicine is just as fragmented, and nobody proposes abolishing it; fragmentation is “more an organisational principle than a pathology.”

More importantly, the field’s core question — why are some firms different? — does not go away if the field does. It gets outsourced: to economists, for whom the firm is a production function; to consultants; or to AI-generated two-by-twos. The trade-off between creating and capturing value is strategy’s own, they argued, and the problems the proposition described are a case for reform, not abolition.

How the room moved

About 60% of the room supported the motion at the start. That fell to roughly 57% after the opening speeches and to around 53% during questions — momentum was moving towards the opposition — but the motion still carried.

The vote is less a verdict than a mood reading. A room of strategy scholars, asked whether their field should exist, was split close to the middle. That seems like a good place for the 2027 Witan to pick up the conversation.
